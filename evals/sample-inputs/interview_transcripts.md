# User interview excerpts — Budgeting app "early access" cohort

Four 1:1 interviews about how people track spending. Excerpts only (lightly trimmed for length).

## Interview A
"I check my balance like five times a day but I never actually log anything. Categorizing every
coffee is just... no. If it doesn't do it for me automatically I'm not going to do it."
"I tried [other app] and it kept asking me to confirm transactions and I just stopped opening it."
"What I actually want to know is — am I okay this month or not? Just tell me that."

## Interview B
"I'm pretty hands-on. I like seeing where every dollar goes, I do a weekly review on Sundays."
"The auto-categorization is wrong a lot though — it put my rent under 'shopping' and I had to fix
it. After the third time I kind of stopped trusting the numbers."
"I'd love a way to set rules, like 'this merchant is always groceries'."

## Interview C
"Honestly I downloaded it, connected my bank, then got a little freaked out seeing everything in one
place and didn't go back for like two weeks."
"It's not that it's hard, it's that it feels like homework. And a bit stressful."
"When I did come back the dashboard had this big red 'over budget' thing and I was like, great,
thanks, I know."

## Interview D
"I share finances with my partner so the single-user thing is a dealbreaker honestly. We both need
to see it."
"The categories don't match how we think — we have 'kid stuff' which is daycare plus clothes plus
random school fees, and there's no way to make that."
"Auto-categorization being off was annoying but I expected it, every app does that."
