# Moderated usability test — Checkout redesign (prototype v3)

Sessions: 5 participants, remote moderated, ~30 min each. Tasks on a clickable prototype.
Tasks: (T1) Add item to cart, (T2) Apply a discount code, (T3) Complete checkout with saved card,
(T4) Change shipping address mid-checkout.

## P1
- T1: success, direct. Fast.
- T2: success but indirect — looked for the code field on the cart page first, then found it on the
  payment page. "Wait, where do I put the code... oh it's down here."
- T3: success.
- T4: FAILED. Couldn't find how to change address after reaching payment; gave up. "I'd just call
  support at this point honestly."
- SEQ for T4: 2/7.

## P2
- T1: success.
- T2: success, direct.
- T3: success but hesitated at the CVV field. "Is this the 3 digits on the back?"
- T4: success but indirect — went back to cart, then re-entered the flow. Took a while.
- Comment: "The code thing tripped me up too, I almost missed it."

## P3
- T1: success.
- T2: FAILED — never found the discount field, checked out without applying it, didn't notice.
- T3: success.
- T4: success, direct.
- SEQ for T2: 3/7. "I assumed there'd be a promo box right in the cart."

## P4
- T1: success.
- T2: success but indirect (found field on payment page after scrolling).
- T3: FAILED — payment button looked disabled (greyed styling), thought the form was broken,
  didn't realize a required field was empty. "Nothing's happening when I click."
- T4: success.

## P5
- T1: success.
- T2: success, direct.
- T3: success.
- T4: success but hesitated; "took me a sec to see the edit link, it's pretty small."
- Overall comment: "Mostly smooth, just the promo code placement is weird."

## Moderator notes
- Discount code placement (T2) came up repeatedly.
- T4 (change address) has no clear entry point once in payment.
- The disabled-looking pay button (P4) may be a visual affordance issue.
